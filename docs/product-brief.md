# Product brief

Status: draft for review.

## Purpose

Help a technical learner understand concepts, inspect how other engineers apply them, and build independently. A working AI-generated artifact is not sufficient evidence of understanding.

## Confirmed scope

- Personal use, one canonical deployed Hermes backend/profile. Ad hoc educational chat is the default; persisting discovery or progress requires explicit tracked-learning opt-in.
- Desktop and web interfaces, with tools and skills usable in ordinary Hermes conversations, including messaging clients.
- Learning records and artifacts persist on the backend and remain available across clients and sessions.
- Both source-first and goal-first entry points, with guided discovery for vague interests and complete beginners. The learner need not know the desired outcome or supply materials.
- Books, papers, repositories, and practical projects are complementary sources.
- Visualization and usable source navigation are core requirements.
- Humanizer is required in the teaching-skill bundle. Explanations must be concrete, direct, and faithful to technical sources, without canned praise or filler.

## Proposed learning loop

Ordinary questions and exploration can stay in chat with no plugin learning-record writes. Start or resume tracked learning explicitly when a continuing record is useful; promotion imports only approved context. Within that opted-in context, begin with a discovery conversation when needed: understand the interest, offer orientation, agree on a provisional brief, and recommend verified starting materials. Accept uncertainty and allow a suggested starting point without a long questionnaire. Then agree on an objective, inspect a source, explain or predict, experiment or implement, record evidence, and revisit later. Recheck the direction after the first activity. Allow the learner to request a direct explanation or change direction. Teaching order is configurable per activity; no default teaching preference has been selected. Assessable activities have criteria before attempts. Preserve assistance events and answer provenance; UI submissions can await later assessment.

## Success

Resume a track in another client without reconstructing it from chat. Trace explanations to sources. Demonstrate conceptual reasoning and practical ability separately. Correct unreliable assessments. Use visuals to investigate behavior.

## Exclusions

Multi-user accounts, classrooms, collaboration, billing, offline writes, arbitrary generated-code execution, and a general replacement for Hermes chat.
